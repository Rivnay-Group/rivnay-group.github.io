Join the group

Prospective students

You apply to a department, not to the group, so start with the department's own instructions. Once you have applied, or if you are already at Northwestern, email Prof. Rivnay with your CV and a short note on your research interests.

PhD

Most of our PhD students come through Biomedical Engineering, where Prof. Rivnay holds his primary appointment, or Materials Science and Engineering. Both take applications in the autumn for the following September, with deadlines in early December; the GRE is not reviewed. Check the department page for that year's exact date.

Master's

We host master's students who want to do research. Apply to the MS in Biomedical Engineering and choose the thesis track, which is the one that carries out a project in a lab. A course-only master's will not place you in the group.

Northwestern undergraduates

Email a CV and your availability. Openings depend on current projects and on who is free to mentor. If you are thinking further ahead, McCormick's combined BS/MS program lets you earn a master's alongside your bachelor's, including in Biomedical Engineering and in Materials Science and Engineering.

Postdoctoral research associate: biohybrid systems and oxygenation

Northwestern University, McCormick School of Engineering
Location: Evanston, IL
Appointment: 1 year minimum, target 2–3 years (renewable upon performance and funding availability)
Start date: as soon as possible

Position overview

We invite applications for a postdoctoral researcher to design, develop, and lead key pre-clinical studies associated with oxygenation technologies, electrochemical actuation in vivo, and biohybrid systems such as “living pharmacies.”

Responsibilities

  • Design and build mechanical and front-end actuation systems
  • Prototyping and rapid design cycles
  • Electrochemical characterization
  • Collaborate closely with materials scientists and biologists to interface electronics with tissue/cells
  • Develop and maintain experimental setups, documentation, and data pipelines
  • Publish findings in peer-reviewed journals and present results at conferences
  • Mentor graduate and undergraduate researchers as part of a collaborative interdisciplinary team

Required qualifications

  • PhD in Biomedical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Materials Science and Engineering, or a related discipline
  • Experience with wearable or implantable bioelectronic systems
  • Demonstrated ability to prototype and debug integrated hardware/software systems
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills

Preferred qualifications

  • Knowledge or expertise in electrochemistry
  • Embedded systems and electronics design and assembly, firmware
  • Cell culture experience, prior animal work

Application materials

  • Brief email cover letter
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Names and contact information for three professional references

Salary and benefits

Salary will follow Northwestern and NIH postdoctoral guidelines and will be commensurate with experience. Full university benefits are included.

How to apply

Send all materials as a single PDF to jrivnay@northwestern.edu with the subject line “Postdoc Application – Biohybrid systems and Oxygenation.”

Postdoctoral research associate: polymer synthesis for organic bioelectronics

Northwestern University, McCormick School of Engineering
Location: Evanston, IL
Appointment: 1 year minimum, target 2–3 years (renewable upon performance and funding availability)
Start date: as soon as possible

Position overview

We invite applications for a postdoctoral researcher to lead the design and synthesis of new conjugated polymers and organic mixed ionic-electronic conductors (OMIECs) for bioelectronic applications. The position works closely with device, biology, and external collaborators.

Responsibilities

  • Design and synthesize conjugated polymers and monomers, using methods such as Stille, Suzuki, direct arylation, and oxidative polymerizations, with side-chain engineering (e.g., glycolated and ionic side chains)
  • Purify materials and characterize their structure and properties (NMR, GPC, FTIR, UV-Vis-NIR, DSC/TGA)
  • Carry out electrochemical and spectroelectrochemical characterization
  • Establish structure–property relationships that govern mixed ionic-electronic transport
  • Collaborate closely with device engineers, biologists, and external partners to supply materials and translate them into devices
  • Contribute to the management of the synthesis laboratory, including chemical inventory, safety, and shared equipment
  • Publish findings in peer-reviewed journals and present results at conferences
  • Mentor graduate and undergraduate researchers as part of a collaborative interdisciplinary team

Required qualifications

  • PhD in Chemistry, Materials Science and Engineering, Chemical Engineering, or a related discipline
  • Strong background in synthetic organic and/or polymer chemistry, ideally with conjugated materials
  • Experience with air- and moisture-sensitive techniques (Schlenk line, glovebox)
  • Excellent written and oral communication skills

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience with OMIECs, OECTs, or other organic electronic devices
  • Expertise in electrochemistry and spectroelectrochemistry
  • Thin-film processing and characterization (e.g., GIWAXS, AFM)

Application materials

  • Brief email cover letter
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Names and contact information for three professional references

Salary and benefits

Salary will follow Northwestern and NIH postdoctoral guidelines and will be commensurate with experience. Full university benefits are included.

How to apply

Send all materials as a single PDF to jrivnay@northwestern.edu with the subject line “Postdoc Application – Polymer synthesis for organic bioelectronics.”
