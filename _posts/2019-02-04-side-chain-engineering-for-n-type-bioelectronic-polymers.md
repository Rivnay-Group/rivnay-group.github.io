A collaborative effort with *Iain McCulloch* (KAUST/Imperial) shows the importance of side-chain engineering for tuning the transistor operating regimes and electrochemical properties of promising electron-transporting polymers. Check out *“The Role of the Side Chain on the Performance of N-type Conjugated Polymers in Aqueous Electrolytes”* out now in [***Chemistry of Materials***](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acs.chemmater.8b00321).
