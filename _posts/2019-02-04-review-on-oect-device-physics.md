A new review paper is out in ***Organic Electronics***, “Device Physics of Organic Electrochemical Transistors” – an opus by Jacob Friedlein. Unlike other reviews on OECTs that focus on chemistry of materials, or give a broad overview of applications, this review highlights aspects related to device physics: scaling behaviors, modeling and disorder, and non-idealities related to parasitic resistance. A must-read for any OECT aficionado.

Download it [here](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1566119918304683) – open access.
