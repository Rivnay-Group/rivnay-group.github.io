Jonathan Rivnay has been named a 2019 Sloan Fellow in Chemistry, awarded by the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation. This award will continue to fund our work developing and understanding organic mixed conductors, and will allow us to pilot new concepts in applications in organic bioelectronic regenerative engineering and neural interfacing.

Follow the links for the formal [press release](https://sloan.org/storage/app/media/programs/SRF/2019-SRF-Press-Release.pdf) from the Sloan Foundation, as well as the [full list of this year’s fellows](https://sloan.org/fellowships/2019-Fellows).

You can also read the coverage from [Northwestern](https://news.northwestern.edu/stories/2019/02/three-northwestern-professors-named-sloan-research-fellows/) and [Northwestern Engineering](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/news/articles/2019/02/jonathan-rivnay-named-sloan-research-fellow.html).
