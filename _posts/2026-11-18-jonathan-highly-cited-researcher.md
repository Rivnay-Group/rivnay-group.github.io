Jonathan Rivnay has been named to Clarivate's Highly Cited Researchers 2026 list in the Cross-Field category. The annual list recognizes researchers whose multiple papers rank in the top 1% by citations for their field and publication year in the Web of Science over the past 11 years. Highly Cited Researchers make up about 1 in 1,000 of the world's scientists and social scientists.

Jonathan is one of 16 Northwestern Engineering faculty on this year's list.

Congratulations, Jonathan!
