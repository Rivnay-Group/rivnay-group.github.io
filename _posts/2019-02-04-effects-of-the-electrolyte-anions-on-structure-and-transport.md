Our paper “Role of the Anion on the Transport and Structure of Organic Mixed Conductors” is published in Advanced Functional Materials. Congratulations to Camila and the entire team. This article highlights the effects of the anion on structure, self-doping, and thus transport and device performance using a suite of electrochemical, structural, spectroscopic and gravimetric probes. [Read it online](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/adfm.201807034).

A continued multinational collaboration with the McCulloch, Salleo, and Inal groups!
