# Site audit, 2026-09-16

Findings from a 114-agent review of the live site (12 reviewers with different lenses, one dedup pass, two independent verifiers per finding, one completeness critic). Every finding below was reproduced by a verifier and checked against the owner decisions recorded in John's memory notes. The plan that acts on it is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-16-site-audit-fixes.md`, which refers to the findings by the C*n*/I*n* numbers used here.

Impact scale. **high**: most visitors notice it, it blocks a task, or it is visibly broken on the live site. **med**: a subset of visitors (one page or one device class), or a maintenance problem that will bite the next editor. **low**: cosmetic or hygiene.

Scratchpad paths quoted in the evidence (`/private/tmp/claude-501/.../scratchpad/...`) were session-only screenshots and scripts and no longer exist; the measurements quoted from them stand.
